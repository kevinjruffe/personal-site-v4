Netlix’s [_The Dinosaurs_](<https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Dinosaurs_(miniseries)>) was a huge hit in our house. My son Conall is still a bit young to pay attention to long shows or movies, but this one had him rapt. Probably just the right combination of subject matter and terror to keep him on his toes.

I can’t recommend this one enough for a family watch if your kiddo loves dinosaurs. Though, like us, you might be asked to skip forward through the some of the “dinocide” scenes!
